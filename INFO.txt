⚡ FEATURES:

   ✅ Draw molecules with atoms and bonds
   ✅ 5 bond types (single, double, triple, wedge, dash)
   ✅ 10 common atoms (C, H, O, N, S, P, F, Cl, Br, I)
   ✅ 8 functional groups (OH, CHO, COOH, NH2, etc.)
   ✅ Quick structures (chains, rings, benzene)
   ✅ 6 molecule templates
   ✅ Reaction mechanism arrows
   ✅ 5 predefined reactions
   ✅ Synthesis pathway tracker
   ✅ Real-time molecular properties
   ✅ Export PNG images
   ✅ Save/load synthesis pathways
   ✅ Undo/redo system

⌨️  KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS:

   Ctrl+Z ......... Undo
   Ctrl+Y ......... Redo
   Ctrl+S ......... Export PNG
   Delete ......... Remove selected atom

💡 EXAMPLES:

   1. Quick benzene ring:
      - Click "⬢ Benzene" button
      - Instant aromatic ring!
   
   2. Draw ethanol:
      - Draw 2 carbons
      - Select one carbon
      - Click "-OH (Alcohol)"
   
   3. Create synthesis:
      - Draw molecule
      - Click "➕ Add Step"
      - Add reaction arrow
      - Draw product
      - Repeat
